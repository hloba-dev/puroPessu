The sofa is the hardest-working piece of furniture in most homes. We sit on it every evening, snack on it, nap on it and watch films on it with kids and pets. Yet it gets cleaned less often than almost any other textile in the house. Sheets go in the wash every week; sofa fabric can go years without a proper clean.

So how often should a sofa be cleaned? There's no single right answer, but there are clear rules of thumb. Below you'll find guidelines for different situations, the signs that cleaning is due, and how to keep the sofa in good shape in between.

## Why a sofa gets dirty even when it looks clean

Most of the dirt in a sofa isn't visible. The fabric constantly collects:

- skin oils and sweat, especially on the armrests, the top of the backrest and where heads rest
- skin flakes and dust, which feed dust mites
- crumbs and drink splashes
- street dust and grit carried in from outside
- pet hair, dander and skin oils.

Oil is the key factor. It binds dust, so the fabric darkens so gradually that you don't notice the change. Many people are surprised after cleaning to see what colour their sofa used to be.

## Recommended cleaning intervals

The table below is a guide. The more rows that apply to your home, the shorter the interval should be.

| Household | Professional cleaning |
|---|---|
| Adults only, no pets, sofa lightly used | Every 1–2 years |
| Typical household, sofa in daily use | About once a year |
| Family with children | 1–2 times a year |
| Dog or cat allowed on the sofa | About twice a year |
| Allergies or asthma in the family | About twice a year |
| Light fabric, or rental-flat furniture before moving out | As needed |

Regular cleaning isn't only about looks. Removing oil and grit from the fibres in good time slows down wear, so the sofa stays looking good for longer.

<Callout type="tip" title="Clean before the dirt shows">
Regular cleaning is easier and gives better results than infrequent cleaning, because oil and dirt don't have time to set into the fabric. It's easy to remember if you tie it to a fixed moment, such as spring cleaning or getting the home ready for Christmas.
</Callout>

## Signs it's time for cleaning

You don't need to wait for the interval in the table if the sofa itself is telling you:

- **The armrests and headrest area have darkened** or feel slick and greasy.
- **The seat has gone grey** compared with the rest of the fabric.
- **The sofa smells**, especially when it's warm or when someone sits down.
- **There are several stains** that home remedies haven't shifted.
- **Allergy symptoms get worse** when you sit or lie on the sofa.
- **The fabric feels stiff or rough** when it should be soft.
- **You're moving, selling or expecting guests** and want the sofa looking its best.

## What to do between cleanings

Everyday care stretches the interval considerably. The essentials:

1. **Vacuum weekly** with the upholstery nozzle, including under cushions, along seams and in the gap by the backrest.
2. **Flip and rotate cushions** regularly if they're removable, so wear and soiling are spread more evenly.
3. **Treat stains right away.** Blot, don't rub. Use cold water and, if needed, a mild detergent solution. Always test on a hidden spot first.
4. **Use washable throws** on the favourite spots of kids and pets.
5. **Protect the sofa from the sun.** Direct sunlight fades fabric, especially next to a window.
6. **Wipe a leather sofa with a damp cloth** and use a leather care product a few times a year. See [leather sofa cleaning](/en/leather-sofa-cleaning) for more.

<Callout type="warning" title="Go easy on water at home">
Cleaning at home with a wet cloth or lots of detergent tends to leave rings and residue that attract new dirt. Apply water sparingly and blot thoroughly.
</Callout>

## In short

- In a typical home, have your sofa professionally cleaned about once a year.
- With kids, pets or allergies in the family, about twice a year is a good interval.
- Darkened armrests, a greying seat and odour are clear signs it's time.
- Weekly vacuuming, quick stain treatment and washable throws extend the interval.
- Regular cleaning keeps the sofa looking good and helps the fabric last longer.
