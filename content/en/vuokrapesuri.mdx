You can rent a carpet and upholstery cleaner (tekstiilipesuri) from many hardware stores and rental shops in Finland, and the idea is appealing: pick up the machine, clean the sofa and the rugs in one go, return it the next day. Often that is exactly how it goes. Sometimes, though, you end up with a soaking sofa, water rings and a sour smell that lingers for a week.

This article is not trying to talk you out of renting. The aim is to be honest about what it really involves, so you can decide with your eyes open.

## What the machine actually is

A rental cleaner is an extraction machine. It sprays water and detergent into the fabric and sucks it straight back out. The principle is the same as professional equipment, but the practical differences are big:

- **Suction power** on consumer rental units is usually much lower, so more water stays in the fabric.
- **Most machines do not heat the water.** You fill them with warm tap water that cools quickly.
- **The tools** are often designed for carpets. A hand tool for upholstery is not always included.

## The real cost and time

| Cost item | Rough figure |
|---|---|
| Machine rental | roughly 30–40 € per day |
| Detergent | roughly 10–25 € per bottle, often only sold by the rental shop |
| Upholstery hand tool | sometimes extra |
| Transport | your own car or a rented van, the machines are heavy |

Allow plenty of time: picking up and returning the machine, filling and emptying the tank several times, the cleaning itself, and cleaning the machine before you return it. A three-seater sofa can easily take half a day. Drying after a rental machine usually takes noticeably longer than after professional extraction, sometimes a full day or more if ventilation is poor.

## The most common mistakes

### Too much water

By far the most common problem. When a stain will not shift, people go over it again and again. Water runs through the fabric into the filling, and weak suction cannot pull it back out. A wet filling dries slowly and can start to smell musty or even mouldy.

### Too much detergent

"A bit extra to be safe" leads to residue. Detergent left in the fabric feels sticky and attracts new dirt fast. Many people find the sofa looks dirtier a few weeks later than before it was cleaned.

### Water rings and edges

When you clean just one stain or one cushion, dirt and dissolved dye collect at the line between wet and dry fabric. Once dry, it becomes a ring that is often more visible than the original stain.

### Ignoring the fabric type

Check the code on the care label first: W and WS fabrics can be wet-cleaned, S and X cannot. Linen, viscose and velvet can shrink or suffer even when the code looks fine.

<Callout type="warning" title="Not for leather">
A rental machine is not suitable for leather sofas, genuine suede or nubuck. Water can leave permanent marks on leather and make it stiff.
</Callout>

## How to get it right if you do it yourself

1. **Vacuum thoroughly** first. Wet dust turns into mud.
2. **Test** both water and detergent on a hidden spot and let it dry.
3. **Use the recommended amount of detergent or less,** never more.
4. **Make light passes,** and after each wet pass do several dry passes with suction only.
5. **Clean whole surfaces** from seam to seam, not individual spots.
6. **Rinse with clean water** at the end if the machine allows it.
7. **Dry actively:** open a window, run a fan or a dehumidifier, and do not sit on the sofa until it is dry.

<Callout type="tip" title="Start in the morning">
Clean in the morning rather than the evening. The sofa gets a full day to dry, and you will spot any rings before they set.
</Callout>

## When renting makes sense, and when it does not

Renting is a reasonable choice when:

- the fabric is W-coded, dark and only lightly soiled
- you also want to freshen up rugs
- you have time, a car and good drying conditions
- you are happy with "fresher" rather than "like new".

A professional is the better call when:

- the sofa is light-coloured and any ring would show immediately
- the stains are old or there are many of them
- there are odours such as urine or smoke that need proper [odor removal](/en/odor-removal)
- the fabric is delicate or you are unsure of the code
- it is a mattress, which is hard to dry after a consumer machine.

## In short

- A rental machine can work well, but the total cost and time are higher than the rental price suggests.
- The main mistakes are too much water, too much detergent and spot cleaning.
- Check the fabric and test on a hidden area before you start.
- Clean whole surfaces, extract until dry and ventilate well.
- Light sofas, old stains, odours and delicate fabrics are best left to a professional.
